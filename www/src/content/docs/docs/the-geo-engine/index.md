This chapter explains how Geo Engine works.
The [operator](/docs/operators/) and [Python](/docs/python/) references assume these concepts.

- [Architecture](/docs/the-geo-engine/architecture): the components of Geo Engine and how they fit together.
- [Processing Graphs](/docs/the-geo-engine/processing-graphs): how computations are described as trees of operators.
- [Queries and Streams](/docs/the-geo-engine/queries-and-streams): how a processing graph is evaluated lazily and returns its result as a stream of tiles or chunks.
- [Time](/docs/the-geo-engine/time): how every piece of data carries a time interval.
- [Raster and Vector Data](/docs/the-geo-engine/raster-and-vector-data): bands, spatial grids, feature collections and columns.
- [Spatial References](/docs/the-geo-engine/spatial-references): coordinate systems and explicit reprojection.
- [Datasets](/docs/the-geo-engine/datasets), [Layers](/docs/the-geo-engine/layers) and [Data Connectors](/docs/the-geo-engine/data-connectors): where data comes from and how it is organized.
- [Users and Permissions](/docs/the-geo-engine/users-and-permissions): sessions, roles, permissions and quotas.
